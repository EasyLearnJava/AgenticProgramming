# Mapleford Community Pool Guide
Mapleford Community Pool is a fictional pool in Mapleford, Oregon. This guide is sample data for testing a RAG application.

## Opening hours
The pool is open from 6am to 9pm Monday through Friday and from 8am to 6pm on Saturday and Sunday. The pool closes for cleaning every Wednesday from 1pm to 3pm.

## Entry fees
A single swim costs $5 for adults and $3 for children under 16. Children under 3 swim free. A monthly pass costs $45.

## Children
Children under 10 must be watched by an adult in the water at all times. Children aged 10 to 15 may swim alone after passing a swim test at the front desk.

## Pool rules
Everyone must shower before entering the pool. Running, diving in the shallow end and glass bottles are not allowed. Food may only be eaten in the café area.
