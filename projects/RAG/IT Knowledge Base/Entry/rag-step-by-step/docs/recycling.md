# Mapleford Recycling and Waste Guide
Mapleford's waste service is fictional. This guide is sample data for testing a RAG application.

## Collection days
Household rubbish is collected every Monday. Recycling is collected every other Thursday. Garden waste is collected on the first Friday of each month from March to November.

## What goes in the blue bin
Paper, cardboard, glass bottles and jars, metal cans and plastic bottles go in the blue bin. Rinse containers first. Plastic bags, food waste and broken glass must not go in the blue bin.

## Batteries and electronics
Batteries and electronics must never go in any bin. Take them to the recycling center on Oak Street, which is open Tuesday to Saturday from 9am to 4pm.

## Public holidays
When a collection day falls on a public holiday, collection moves to the next day.
