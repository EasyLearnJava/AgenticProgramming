# Maple Grove Community Library Guide
Maple Grove Community Library is a fictional library in Mapleford, Oregon. This guide is sample data for testing a RAG application.

## Opening hours
The library is open from 9am to 8pm Monday through Thursday and from 10am to 6pm on Friday and Saturday. The library is closed on Sundays, New Year's Day, and Labor Day.

## Laptop loans
Members aged 18 or older may borrow a library laptop for up to two hours. Laptops may only be used inside the library and cannot be taken home.

## Public computers
Each visitor may use a public computer for 60 minutes per day. If nobody is waiting, staff may approve one additional 30-minute session. Members who sign in to a public computer need to update credentials every Friday at the information desk.

## Food and drinks
Food and drinks are not allowed in the reading rooms, except for water in closed bottles. Visitors may consume other food and drinks in the outdoor courtyard.
