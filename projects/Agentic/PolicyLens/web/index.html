THE SOURCE OF TRUTH

Your knowledge base.

A clear view of what your assistant can actually read.

Document collection

Starter documents are an attributed snapshot of the public GitLab handbook. Refresh with python scripts/fetch_corpus.py, then rebuild. A matching filename and extension replaces the active revision; older bytes remain in local history. Removed uploads can be restored. Rebuild after changes.
