How to read the scores

Keyword score is the raw BM25-style score. Keyword contribution compresses it with keyword / (1 + keyword).

Semantic score is cosine similarity using all {{ catalog.dimensions }} vector dimensions, from −1 to 1. “Not available” means it was not computed, rather than a similarity of zero.

Final score = keyword contribution + semantic score. A missing semantic score contributes nothing. In keyword-only mode, the final score is just the keyword contribution. Scores are not probabilities.

A chunk qualifies with a positive keyword score or semantic similarity of at least {{ diagnostics.semantic_threshold }}. Qualified chunks are ranked by final score, with chunk ID breaking ties. Chat takes up to {{ diagnostics.max_passages }} chunks within its character budget.
