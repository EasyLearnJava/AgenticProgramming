How to read the scores

Keyword score is the raw BM25-style score, based on matching terms, rarity and passage length. Each retrieval method ranks its qualifying passages independently.

Semantic score is cosine similarity from −1 to 1. “Not returned” means the hosted search did not return that candidate; “Not available” means no score is available for this run. Neither means a measured zero.

Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) combines those ranks: BM25 contributes {{ diagnostics.keyword_weight }} / ({{ diagnostics.rrf_k }} + rank); semantic contributes {{ diagnostics.semantic_weight }} / ({{ diagnostics.rrf_k }} + rank). Without a BM25 match, a semantic score must also reach {{ diagnostics.standalone_semantic_threshold }} before it contributes. Final score = keyword contribution + semantic contribution. Keyword-only results use the keyword contribution. Scores measure relevance, not correctness or confidence.

Semantic ranking starts at {{ diagnostics.semantic_threshold }}. A chunk qualifies for the answer with a positive keyword score or semantic similarity of at least {{ diagnostics.standalone_semantic_threshold }}. Qualified chunks are ranked by final score, with chunk ID breaking ties. Chat takes up to {{ diagnostics.max_passages }} chunks within its character budget.
