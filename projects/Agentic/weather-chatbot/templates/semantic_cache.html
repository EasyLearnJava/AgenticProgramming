What these times mean

The two-minute limit starts when an answer is saved. It can expire sooner if its weather data expires or the date changes. Reusing an answer does not extend its lifetime.

This page shows normalized weather labels, not your original questions, answers or saved preferences. Trip plans, park guidance, safety advice and alerts are excluded from this answer cache.

Open-Meteo’s weather-data cache is separate. It can save a provider request even when Gemini writes a new answer.
