You choose what stays

Mem0 stores only preferences you explicitly save here or confirm from a chat preview. Nimbus does not send your full conversation to memory.

  • Weather, alerts, prices and availability are checked again for each relevant request.
  • New chat clears the conversation, but keeps these saved defaults.
  • Trip dates, guests and booking permissions are not saved as preferences.

Saved for this browser

This is a browser profile, not an account. A different browser or device starts separately. Anyone using this browser profile can view or change these preferences.

The browser link and preferences expire after 180 days from saving. Expiry stops normal use; it does not guarantee removal from Mem0's storage. Use Forget below to request deletion.

Clearing browser cookies can lose access to your saved preferences. Forget them first if you want them deleted.
